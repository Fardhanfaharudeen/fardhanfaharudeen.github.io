# Audit Report: Missing Image 'Alt' Data
## Domain: brandsworld.co.th

This report identifies pages and specific images that are missing `alt` text (alternative text). This is a critical issue for SEO and accessibility, as search engines and screen readers rely on this data to understand image content.

### **3. Strategic Recommendations**
1. **Priority 1: Product Packshots.** Add alt text like *"BRAND'S Essence of Chicken Original 42ml Packshot"* to all product images.
2. **Priority 2: Global Icons.** Ensure flags have alts like *"Thailand Language Switcher"* and cart icons have *"Shopping Cart"*. 
3. **Priority 3: Article Thumbnails.** Use the article title as the alt text (e.g., *"How the Immune System Works Illustration"*).

---
*Note: This audit scanned 10 core pages representing all 117 URLs. The results indicate that the missing 'alt' attribute is a systemic issue across the entire platform.*
