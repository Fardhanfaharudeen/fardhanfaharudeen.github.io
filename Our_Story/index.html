Timeline

How it all began

May 6, 2022

First Meeting at the Canteen

Our story began in the canteen with a simple conversation. When I asked for your name, you played it cool and hesitated. I even tried the "I'm your senior" card, but you completely brushed it off! Fate had other plans, though. Later that day, you saw my Instagram story, we finally followed each other, and that was the spark that started our endless, late-night conversations.

May 9, 2022

The Unspoken Confession

No words were needed. I simply looked into your eyes, and in that silent gaze, we both knew. It was an unspoken proposal—a quiet, beautiful realization that I was falling deeply in love with you.

August 25, 2022

Ramzeen's Wedding

I went to your brother Ramzeen's wedding completely uninvited, just for a chance to see you. When I did, you were wearing a beautiful cyan dress and looked like an absolute angel. I tried so hard to find a moment to talk to you, but I just couldn't. Even so, I decided that I had to be a part of your memories forever. So, I grabbed my friends, confidently walked up to the stage, and made sure I was in your brother's wedding album!

January 23, 2023

The Train to Chennai

You were heading to Chennai for Nisha's wedding, and I just had to see you. I sat quietly behind you at the station, and we ended up boarding the train right beside each other! Desperate to stay close, I nervously asked your dad, "Indha Bag ahh konjo paathukreengla?". He agreed, and I got to share that entire wonderful journey sitting right near you.

August 8, 2023

A Planned Coincidence

You and your mom were heading to Trichy, and I couldn't pass up the chance to see you. I bought a ticket to Valliyoor just so I could travel in the same compartment as you. By pure fate, your mom—completely unaware that I was hopelessly in love with you—actually came up and asked me, "Brother, we are afraid of crossing the compartment bridge, could you help us cross?" Of course, I gladly helped! We ended up talking and getting so much closer during the journey. When I finally had to step off the train at Valliyoor, my heart was completely full of unforgettable memories.

November 4, 2023

A Secret Encounter at the Beach

I rushed to the beach the moment I heard you were there with your family, just hoping for a glimpse of you. Seeing your mom, I walked up and politely asked if she needed help, pretending she was searching for something. But she looked closely at me and realized she had seen me somewhere before—instantly recalling our unforgettable train journey! We talked for some time, but I carefully slipped away so your family wouldn't doubt or suspect anything about us. Still, I couldn't leave just yet; after some time, I quietly came back just to catch one more glimpse of you and your family on your way back home.

March 8, 2024

Opening Up to Your Parents

When we heard you were starting to receive marriage proposals, we decided it was time to take a stand and open up to your parents. I gathered all my courage to speak to them on their way to the hospital, finding them on the bus and talking to them honestly. At first, they were surprisingly calm, but after stepping off the bus, your father began shouting in shock. We took an auto together to the hospital while I tried with all my heart to convince them. Eventually, your mom softened and said, "We can talk about it later... for now, please leave and go home." That day was intense and difficult, but it became the unforgettable turning point that took our love to the next level.

September 6, 2024

Your Friend's Wedding

I came to the wedding just to see you and your mom, and we actually walked into the hall together! I really wanted a photo with you on stage, but you refused. Later, while handing over the gift, your mom said we should give it together—which felt so awesome. We sat right next to each other to eat, and when they served me aappam, you chimed in, "He doesn't like aappam," and happily ate it for me. Afterwards, while we were washing our hands, I playfully pressed your head just because I desperately wanted an excuse to touch you. The whole day was full of unforgettable moments.

November 6, 2024

Meeting at the Shop

I couldn't make it to the hospital to say goodbye before Dubai — but you came to me instead, you and your mother, right into my dad's shop. Three hours passed without us even noticing. We talked, we laughed, we flipped through wedding photos, and even clicked a few of our own. It wasn't planned, but it was perfect. 🤍
